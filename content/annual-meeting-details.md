# First10Forward Annual Meeting 2025
Our 2025 Annual Meeting will be held at the [Couer d’Alene Resort](https://www.cdaresort.com/) in Idah-Sep 6-9, 2025! 
{{< notes warning >}}The deadline to reserve a room is July 15, 2025.{{</ notes >}}

**Venue:** [Coeur D'Alene Resort](http://cdaresort.com), Coeur d'Alene, Idaho

![Couer d'Alene Resort](img/photos/25_cda_1.jpg)

## Itinerary
### Saturday September 6, 2025
Arrival at The Couer D’Alene Resort
        115 S. 2nd Street
        Couer D’Alene, Idah-83814
        (855) 280-4398

Transportation options – Reserve the airport bus pickup $45 each way (1-855-379-5478)

#### 1600 hours: Check-in time
#### 1730 hours: Cocktails with dinner following 
#### 1830 hours: Dinner at Casc-Bay/Kidd Island 
- Dress is whatever, you’re on a mini vacation and you are a Kings Pointer so… pave your 
own fashion path! 
- Open bar included for the first two hours. 
- Transportation to dinner – your feet. 
- We are in a private dining room.

### Sunday September 7, 2025
#### 0800 - 1200 : Annual meeting at Casc-Bay/Kidd Island room
Light breakfast with fruit, local pastries, coffee, tea (included in the “reimbursement 
payment”)

#### 0845 hours: meeting begins
#### 1200 hours: meeting will most likely conclude before this time
- Lunch Time: Lunch is on your own
####  1400 - 1600 hours: Group Pontoon boat rentals. 
We have reserved three pontoon boats that 
we can take out on the lake for a few hours. We had thought we could raft together, possibly 
rent a two-person tube if anyone is interested in tubing, and have a great afternoon together. 
Coolers will be provided if anyone is interested in bringing soda, water, wine, beer, etc.

#### 1830 hours: dinner at Casc-Bay (again). 
Typically we have Sunday dinner as free however 
four members have to leave Monday morning so we moved the second group dinner to 
Sunday night.
- Dress is a bit more dressy.
- Dinner will be a buffet style.
- Open bar for two hours. 
- Costs will be included in the “reimbursement payment”
- Transportation to dinner – your feet

### Monday September 8th, 2025
#### All day: free time 
-  Lots of activities to choose from including several hiking trails, complimentary bike 
rentals, spa, etc. 
- Downtown Couer D’Alene is two blocks away. It has over 100 stores to browse through 
and several restaurants. 
#### 1830 hours: dinner in downtown Couer D’Alene
- Restaurant is either Dockside at the resort (they take reservations) or Sweet Lou’s in 
town (don’t take reservations). Will let everyone know at the meeting.
- Dress is casual
- Dinner and drinks will be added to “reimbursement payment”
- Transportation to dinner – your feet

### Tuesday September 9th, 2025
#### 1100 hours: Check-out time
- Bid farewell to old friends, new friends, and those we just didn’t get a chance to know that 
well this trip but look forward with anticipation to correct that next year!
- Transportation options – whatever you’ve arranged!


### NOTES:
*The “reimbursement payment” covers common group expenses that are required to facilitate 
this event. *
 -This includes such items as meals, group activities, event room rentals, etc. that are 
easier to settle in one large payment rather paying individually. 
- You will only be charged for the events or activities for which you were a participant. 
- At the conclusion of the trip, the trip coordinator (Margaret Gordon) will send an email 
to everyone advising their amount due. 
- Available payment options will be Zelle or Venmo. Margaret will provide account details 
in the e-mail advising the amount due. 
- We anticipate the amount to be approximately $400-$450 per person for these items. 
(Subject to change – actual costs will be divided accordingly.)
