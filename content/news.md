# First10Forward Awards 2025 Annual Grant to Missouri Women in Trades

**FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE**

**Kings Point, NY – January 15, 2025** – First10Forward, an organization of women graduates from the United States Merchant Marine Academy, has announced Missouri Women in Trades (MOWIT) as the recipient of its 2025 Annual Grant. The grant was awarded following presentations and voting by members at First10Forward's Annual Meeting.

## About the Recipient

Missouri Women in Trades (MOWIT) is a St. Louis-based nonprofit organization dedicated to expanding opportunities for women to enter and succeed in apprenticeships and careers in the construction and building trades. MOWIT works with employers, unions, educational organizations, and other entities to increase women's equal employment opportunity and equitable working conditions while building a diverse workforce and promoting women's economic security.

## MOWIT's Impact

MOWIT provides comprehensive support through multiple programs:

- **Recruitment & Outreach**: Partnership with Girl Scouts for badge workshops, Women in Construction Week activities at area high schools, and community events introducing girls to construction trades through programs like Mentoring A Girl In Construction Summer Camp and Ms Fix It Workshops.

- **Job Placement & Retention**: Connection of tradeswomen to regional jobs through partnerships with construction owners and contractors, including a private Available-For-Work List and direct referrals.

- **Ongoing Support**: Monthly Meet Ups, Survival Skills training, Workdays with MOWIT, and referrals to an independent support group for tradeswomen.

## About First10Forward

First10Forward raises funds through annual memberships sold to eligible classes of women graduates from the United States Merchant Marine Academy and through donations from friends and colleagues who share the organization's passion for helping women and girls achieve their professional goals.

Each year, members nominate charitable organizations from their communities that further First10Forward's mission of supporting women and girls pursuing non-traditional careers. After presentations at the Annual Meeting, members vote on which organization will receive the Annual Grant in its entirety.

"MOWIT exemplifies our mission of supporting women and girls pursuing non-traditional careers," said [Member Name], the nominating member. "Their comprehensive approach to recruitment, training, and ongoing support is creating real pathways for women in the construction trades."

## Looking Forward

The 2025 Annual Grant will support MOWIT's continued efforts to break down barriers for women entering the construction trades and provide the resources and community support needed for long-term career success.
